04 CURRENT FOCUS ／ Where we start

HOSPITALITY ／ INBOUND ／ JAPAN

We start with
hospitality.

Right now, starting from hotels and accommodation in Japan, we are working on new experiences that connect the local to the world.

A hotel is not only a place to sleep. It can be the entrance to meeting a region.

From inside the hotel to the street.
From the street to the region.

We are not a hotel-only company. It is simply the area we have chosen as our starting point.

EXPERIMENTAL PROJECT ／ CONCEPT

One Room Project

One of the first experiments toward realising our vision.

Before moving anything large, we create small — one room, one space, one experience. People actually spend time in it, we watch the response, and expand if it works.

The One Room Project is an experimental project at concept stage, and is not Vitality Design itself. There are no proven results at this point.

05 TECHNOLOGY ／ Technology

Technology should
disappear into
the experience.

AI, software, AR, IoT, projection, sound, lighting. All of them are means for realising an experience.

We do not use technology in order to show technology.

  • To know a place more deeply.
  • To feel it more strongly.
  • To connect people and place.
  • To stay in memory.

In the longer term we are thinking toward a shared technical foundation — Experience OS — on which a different experience can be implemented in each place. This is a concept, not a finished product we offer today.
