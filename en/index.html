Our story ↓
01 — OUR ORIGINOur origin

At the heart of every journey:
people and places.

Travelling across Japan and around the world,
what stayed with me were the people I met
and the time I spent in each place.

A local tip shared by someone at the inn.
A moment of connection with local history and culture.
An ordinary conversation that changed how I saw a place.

Even in the same town, who you meet and how you spend your time
can make a journey entirely different.

Those experiences are where our story begins.

02 — OUR VISIONOur vision

The character of a place.
The spirit of a stay.

Understand what belongs to a place.
Discover what it could become.

The care behind a building or a space.

Culture and skills passed down through generations.

Food, landscapes and the knowledge of those who work there.

The everyday ways of life a community holds dear.

A sense
of place.

The richness of hospitality and travel grows from
connections between people and places.

We explore these qualities and bring them together
to create experiences that reach the people who visit.

03 — THE FUTURE WE SEEKThe future we seek

An encounter.
A lasting
connection.

  1. A memory that stays with the visitor.
  2. Pride and income for the people who welcome them.
  3. A future for local culture and ways of life.

We care about how these things connect.

The exchanges and spending that travel brings can support the next chapter for a property or community. New possibilities grow, giving people a reason to return.

We aim to build businesses that keep this cycle going.
