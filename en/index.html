TOKYO, JAPAN

01 ABOUT ／ About us

Places hold value that has not
yet become an experience.

Every region in Japan has its own history. There are people working there, crafts passed down, food, buildings and landscape.

Each has value on its own, yet in many places none of it is connected into something a person can experience.

Vitality Design finds that value and works through

  • Who is it for?
  • What experience should it become?
  • How does it generate revenue?
  • How is it actually delivered on site?

— and then builds it ourselves, as a business.

What we want to build
is not a proposal document.
It is a business people actually visit,
experience, pay for, and that keeps going.

02 VISION ／ Making travel more of an experience

Making travel more of an experience.

Take Asakusa.

From the moment you enter the hotel, you touch the story of a town that runs back to Edo.The next day, you step out into the town.

  • Long-established shopsLearn why the shop has lasted so long.
  • WorkshopsTouch the craftsman's work, and try making something yourself.
  • MuseumsExperience the next part of the story you met at the hotel.
  • RestaurantsNot just the food, but why these ingredients and this culture took root here.
  • Shrines, alleys,
    markets, riverside    The more you walk, the more each place connects to the next.

Not “I toured Asakusa,”
but “I experienced Asakusa.”
That is the kind of travel we want.

What we want to build is not a theme park contained inside a hotel.

It is a future where the town itself becomes one experience.

04 WHAT WE DO ／ How we build businesses

Building new businesses
from the value of a place.

Vitality Design is not a contractor that only solves the problems a client hands over.

We find the opportunity inside a place or a customer, form our own business hypothesis, validate it with partners, and grow what holds into a business.

01

Understand the site,
the customer and the economics.

UNDERSTAND

From guest feedback, on-site operations, existing equipment and the revenue structure, we find the business opportunity in that place.

02

Design both the experience
and the business.

DESIGN

To whom, what, and at what price. We do not stop at “an interesting experience” — we work through to a business model that can continue.

03

Combine only what
is actually needed.

BUILD

Space, projection, sound, lighting, AI, AR, IoT, web, equipment — we choose the means that fit the purpose. Technology is never the starting point.

04

Implement it
on site.

IMPLEMENT

We form teams with specialist firms, creators, engineers and local businesses as needed, and build it through to actual use.

05

Look at the numbers
and the response.

MEASURE

Revenue, unit price, usage, occupancy, satisfaction — we set KPIs per business and test the hypothesis.

06

Grow it
into a business.

EXPAND

We start with one venue, one space, one region, and roll the proven model out to other venues and regions.

05 WE ARE AN OPERATING COMPANY ／ We are an operating company

We are
an operating company.

We are not a consultancy that only gives advice. We are not a contractor that only produces what it is asked for.

Nor a company that submits a report and stops, nor one that only does interiors, only builds systems, or only installs equipment.

What we do

  4. Gather the partners

  6. Create revenue

06 CURRENT FOCUS ／ Current focus

Starting with accommodation.

Right now we are developing businesses with hotels and accommodation as our first focus area.

Within a trip, a hotel is where a person and a place spend the longest time together.At the same time, we believe there is still great potential between the stay and the region's culture, history and commerce.

From a place you simply sleep
to the entrance to a place.

Themes we are testing now

  • Raising value by drawing on inbound demand
  • New experiences in guest rooms and public areas
  • Renovation and equipment investment in existing properties
  • Building businesses that lift ADR and ancillary revenue
  • Experience design connecting hotels and local resources

All of these are at the hypothesis-testing stage. We do not present any of them as proven results at this point.

Concept visual — not an actual completed project

07 ONE ROOM PROJECT

Testing the future,
one room at a time.

To build a new experience, there is no need to change the whole property from the start.

  1. 01One room first
  2. 02One space first
  3. 03One experience first

Build it small, let real users use it, look at the response and the numbers. If it works, expand.

The One Room Project expresses“build a business small, and confirm it on site”— the way we work.

Projection, sound, lighting, AR, AI and IoT are only options for realising an experience.We start not from what to install, but from what someone should experience there.
