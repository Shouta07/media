01 ABOUT ／ About us

Places hold value that has not
yet become an experience.

A region holds a great deal of value: culture, history, people, craft, food, buildings, landscape.

Yet in many places none of it has been turned into an experience that travellers or users will actually pay for.

Vitality Design connects the value in a place to real customer demand, and turns it intoan experience business people actually visit, pay for, and that keeps going.

What we want to build is not a proposal,
but a business that keeps going.

02 WHAT WE BUILD ／ What we build

Experience Asset

What we build is not a one-off event or a piece of staging.

We build an experience business that keeps raising the brand value and the revenue of that place.

We do not build a space.
We build a new business in a place.

  • 01

    Turning unused hotel space
    into a paid experience business.

    Unused guest rooms, lobbies, basements, rooftops. We redesign floor area as a unit that converts into revenue.

  • 02

    Turning historic buildings into cultural
    experiences that exist only there.

    Preservation alone leaves only the upkeep bill. We turn the building itself into the stage for an experience people pay for.

  • 03

    Turning sake breweries and workshops into
    experiences inbound guests value highly.

    Not a tour that ends at looking. We build the maker's craft and its story into an experience that carries a price.

  • 04

    Connecting a hotel to the cultural assets
    of its town, as one experience brand.

    Rather than ending inside the property, we extend the flow of people and revenue to long-established shops, workshops, cultural venues and restaurants.

03 HOW WE BUILD ／ How we build

From thinking it through
to implementing it on site.

We do not stop at a proposal or a consulting engagement.We work out what should be built, design a form that holds as a business, assemble the partners, implement it on site, and improve it against the numbers.

01

Understand the site, the customer,
the economics and the local assets.

UNDERSTAND

02

Decide who it is for, what it is,
and at what price.

DEFINE

We do not stop at “an interesting experience” — we bring it down to a form where price, cost and continuity add up.

03

Assemble the partners
the business needs.

PARTNER

Hotels, local businesses, creators, technology firms, contractors. We gather whoever each business needs.

04

Combine only the means
is actually needed.

BUILD

Space, projection, sound, lighting, AI, AR, IoT, web, equipment — chosen backwards from the purpose.

05

Implement it until it is used
in the real world.

OPERATE

We do not stop at building it. We take it to a state staff can run and deliver every day.

06

Scale what works
to other venues and regions.

SCALE

We check usage, revenue, unit price and satisfaction, then roll the proven model sideways.

04 TECHNOLOGY ／ Technology

Experience OS

What we are working toward isa technical foundation that lets the experience of a real place be changed, controlled and improved like software.

Projection, sound, lighting, smartphones, sensors, AI, AR, IoT — combined to implement a different experience in each place.

The experience differs with each place.
The machinery behind it can be shared.

Asakusa, Kyoto, Kanazawa — we are working toward running all of them on the same foundation.

  1. EXPERIENCEA different experience in each placeAsakusa ／ Kyoto ／ Kanazawa …
  2. EXPERIENCE OSThe shared technical foundationControl ／ Delivery ／ Measurement
  3. DEVICESWhat sits on siteProjection ／ Sound ／ Lighting ／ Sensors ／ Smartphones

Experience OS is a technical foundation we are currently conceiving and developing. It is not offered as a finished product at this stage. AI, AR and IoT are means, never the goal.

05 BUSINESS MODEL ／ Business model

We do not stop at delivery.

Vitality Design is not a production company that hands over a deliverable and leaves.

We combine different ways of taking revenue, depending on the project.

The more a successful business grows,
the more we grow with it.
That is the structure we are building toward.

Forms we combine

  • Business development feeFor the concept and the business design
  • Implementation feeFor implementation on site
  • Revenue ShareA share of the revenue created
  • IP LicenseLicensing the format and the machinery
  • Joint venturesOperating together, holding economic rights
  • JVForming a joint venture entity

Which form applies is decided case by case. The same terms are not applied to every project.

06 CURRENT FOCUS ／ Current focus

Starting with accommodation.

Hotels and accommodation are our first focus area.

From a place you simply sleep
to the entrance to a place.

We are not a hotel-only company. It is simply the area we have chosen to start in.

Why start with accommodation

  • There is already contact with travellers.
  • There is real physical space.
  • Renovation and equipment investment already happen.
  • Results can be measured: ADR, ancillary revenue, utilisation.
  • It can become the entrance to the surrounding area.
Concept visual — not an actual completed project

07 ONE ROOM PROJECT

The project where we first test the approach

Testing the future,
one room at a time.

Before committing to a large investment,we implement small — one room, one space, one experience.

Let real people use it, check the revenue and the response, and expand if it works.

A room that becomes the story itself.

The One Room Project is not our flagship service. It is the project where we first confirm the approach. There are no proven results at this stage.
