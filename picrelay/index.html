FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Can we order if our company is based outside Japan?

Yes. PICRELAY is designed for international businesses that need photos of specific places within Japan.

Can you photograph any location in Japan?

We accept requests for locations across Japan, but feasibility depends on local contributor availability, access, permission and scheduling.

What will we receive?

Photo files organized by location and a documentation report with image-based observations and relevant capture details. No video or professional inspection is included.

Can you photograph inside a store or unstaffed business?

Only where the appropriate permission is in place. We confirm access arrangements, privacy rules and photography restrictions before accepting an assignment.

Can a photo prove its own location and capture date?

No. Photos and editable metadata alone cannot conclusively prove authenticity. We may use additional records to support checks, without guaranteeing absolute verification.

How much does it cost?

We quote each project based on geography, number of sites, subjects and timing. Tell us what you need and we'll review feasibility.
